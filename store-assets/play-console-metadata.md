### Full Description (≤ 4000 chars)
In an era where anyone can ask AI to generate an app, fake a checklist, or spam hollow praise, **Craft Glass** celebrates the one thing AI cannot replace: **genuine human effort over time**.

Built around an anti-burnout **Weekly Pool** model instead of stressful daily streaks, Craft Glass helps students, indie builders, language learners, and lifelong crafters build lasting habits without cramming.

**CORE PILLARS**

• **Weekly Pool & Anti-Cramming Design**
Set weekly totals (minutes, pages, problems, or sessions — never mixed) with auto-generated micro-chunks and soft weekday pins. Skip a busy day guilt-free: your Weekly Streak tracks weeks reaching 80%+ completion. Weekly Expedition Chests cap daily credit at 90 minutes so steady rhythm beats last-minute cramming.

• **Proof of Human Craft (Blurred Desk Timelapses & Proof Ledger)**
Enable the optional Desk Camera during Focus sessions with 3 levels of real-time privacy blur (Clear, Soft Blur, or Silhouette) baked directly before frames are saved locally. Every recorded session is chained into an on-device SHA-256 Proof Ledger, ready to export as a crisp 1080px Weekly Proof Card PNG or WebM timelapse.

• **Human Blessing Pacts (Zero AI Praise)**
Craft Glass has zero AI cheerleaders. Export a signed **Proof Pact Code (`CGP1:...`)** and share it with a real friend, classmate, family member, or mentor. When they inspect your week and return a signed **Blessing Code (`CGB1:...`)** (protected against self-blessing and replay), your pixel hero gains a 7-day aura.

• **400-Hour Living Pixel Guild & Memory Forge (FSRS)**
- **25 Guild Ranks (Lv.1–Lv.99 over 400 hours)** & **20 Pixel Worlds** (`1 min = 1 EXP`)
- **12 Pixel Companions** whose bond grows solely from real minutes spent focusing together
- **Break Gems** discovered on 5+ minute sessions — claimable only during your 5-minute Break
- **Sealed Monsters & FSRS Memory Forge**: Overdue spaced-repetition flashcards and neglected habits appear as monsters you can re-seal through real review
- **Honest Time Correction**: Adjust forgotten timers anytime; EXP, bonds, chests, gems, and gear reconcile automatically and honestly
- **100% Local-First & Private**: Works completely offline with no accounts, no ads, no subscriptions, and full JSON backup export/import.

毎日ログインや日割りノルマで消耗させるのではなく、Study Glassの「週間プール制・詰め込み防止」哲学を受け継ぎ、世界中の勉強・資格・個人開発・語学・創作に使えるローカルファースト習慣RPGとして再構築しました。

**主な特徴**
• **週間プール＆詰め込み防止**：日割りノルマではなく1週間の総量（分・ページ・問・回）で管理。1日休んでも週間ストリークは切れません。遠征ボーナスは1日90分上限で、一夜漬けより分散継続が報われます。

• **400時間・25段階ギルドRPG＆FSRS記憶定着**：1分=1EXPでLv.99・20ワールドへ進む冒険、5分以上の集中後の「休憩で見つけた宝石」、間隔反復FSRSカードと連携した「封印された魔物」、そして後から正直に直せる「時間修正＆自動精算」を搭載。
